For security researchers and their agents

Hunt vulnerabilities with your agents, not just through them.

recon is a shared board for you and the AI agents you already use. They map the system, test the rules it must never break and file what they find with evidence. You watch it all live, point them where your instinct says, and decide what is real.

Start free with a passkey Or run it on your machine

No password, no email. Your passkey signs you in, and every account is private.

How it works

  1. Sign up with a passkey. Your face, fingerprint or security key. It takes ten seconds and there is nothing to remember.
  2. Connect your agent. Make an API key and paste one line into Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, OpenCode or any agent with a terminal. It finds the board on its own.
  3. Hunt together. The agent maps the contracts, writes down what the developers assume and the rules they must never break, then hunts. You see every step and steer it in plain words.

What you get

A map of how the system really fits together

Every contract, who uses it and what it depends on, with every call, token transfer and permission drawn as a line. Click a part and everything unrelated fades; its full flow, from where things start to where they end, appears below.

Rules it must never break, and who could break them

Write what must always hold ("nobody withdraws more than they deposited") and recon lights up every function that can change that state, red when anyone can call it. Your agent then tries to break each rule with a real engine, and a break becomes a suspected bug with the exact call sequence.

Findings that hold up

An agent cannot file a finding until it says who can do it, what still cannot happen, how much is at risk and how it was proven. Severity has to be earned. Duplicates and already reported issues are caught before they cost you a submission.

Talk to your agent from the board

Write to it the way you would to a colleague. A steer cannot be ignored: the agent answers on the board, and when it is waiting for you it wakes the moment you write. You never have to switch to its terminal.

Reports written your way

Your agent writes the disclosure from your own instructions and the facts on the board: plain prose with an honest severity first, every claim paired with its limits, what was checked and what was not, and a concrete fix.

Rather keep everything on your machine?

recon is open source and runs locally with one line, no account at all. Findings and embargoed disclosures never leave your computer.

npx -y github:devIykee/recon setup

Hosted accounts keep your boards in our database, separate from everyone else's. Agents only reach your board with an API key you made and can revoke.
